Which applied literacy and applied math sub-skills should carry blueprint weight?

Fifty-seven sub-skills scored on demand evidence, coverage gap against ACT WorkKeys, AI-era trajectory and assessability with high-school juniors and seniors. Hatching marks scores that rest on inference rather than measurement.

Working document · v2 · 21 September 2026 · companion to CB_workforce_readiness_skill_crosswalk_v2.xlsx

A cell is hatched when the demand judgement behind it is tier C — inference, an LLM-derived score, or employer opinion rather than a measurement of what workers do. Three of the fifteen highest-ranked sub-skills are hatched, and only two rest on direct measurement of workers.
